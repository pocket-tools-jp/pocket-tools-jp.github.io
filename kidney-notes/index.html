What it does

One-tap daily logging

Sub-Q fluids (with amount presets and injection-site rotation), medications with reminders and stock alerts, weight, appetite, water, vomiting and more — designed to take under 30 seconds a day.

Kidney values, charted

Log CREA, BUN, SDMA, P and Ca from vet visits and see the trend over time. Keep photos of the lab reports too.

A report your vet can read in seconds

Your daily log and lab values become a clean 30/90-day PDF — days with vomiting or low appetite are highlighted. Show it at the clinic or print it.

Paw Prints

A small paw stamp for every day you log. Milestones are celebrated gently — and skipped days are never scolded. Care is hard enough.

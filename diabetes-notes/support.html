How do I log an insulin shot?

Tap Insulin on the Home tab, choose AM or PM, and tap the dose in units — it is logged right away. For other amounts, type the units and tap Log. Times and extra shots can be edited in the Detailed log.

How do I record a BG curve?

Tap BG on the Home tab each time you test. All of the day's readings appear together on the BG curve (Charts → BG curve). Use ◀ ▶ to look at other days.

What is the target band?

It is the BG range you agreed with your veterinarian. Enter it under Charts → BG curve → Change and it appears as a shaded band on the charts. The app has no default or recommended range and never suggests one.

Can I use mmol/L?

Not yet — BG is currently recorded in mg/dL. (To convert, 1 mmol/L ≈ 18 mg/dL.)

Does the app tell me whether a reading or dose is OK?

No. Diabetes Notes is a record-keeping tool. It does not interpret readings, diagnose, or suggest insulin doses. Please follow your veterinarian's instructions.

What is free and what is Premium?

  • Free: every logging feature, reminders, and charts and history for the last 14 days. Your first vet PDF is also free.
  • Premium (optional): 30- and 90-day charts and history, vet-ready PDF export, and more than one cat. Monthly or yearly subscription; the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

How do I cancel or restore Premium?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open More → About Premium → Restore purchases.

Where is my data? How do I move to a new phone?

All data is stored only on your device — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. Before switching phones, use More → Export backup and save the file somewhere safe, then use More → Restore from backup on the new phone. Photos are stored on the original device and may not carry over.

Can I switch between kg and lb?

Yes. More → Weight unit. Your saved weights stay the same; only the unit used for display and entry changes.
